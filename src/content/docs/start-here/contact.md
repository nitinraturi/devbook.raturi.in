## A helpful message format

Use this for email or a direct message. Skip the subject line on social media.

```text
Subject: [Topic] — [page or project]

Hi Nitin,

I'm contacting you about: [brief context]

Page or link (if relevant): [URL]

Message or question: [details]

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

Please do not include passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in a message.
