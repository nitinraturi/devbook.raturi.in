Applies to site copy, docs, blog posts, UI text and release notes.

## Purpose
- Each page answers a real question for a defined reader, and answers it better than what already exists. Give each page one main intent.
- Publish fewer, better pages. Never create near-duplicate pages for keyword variants, doorway pages or mass-generated pages made mainly to rank.

## Voice
- Plain, direct and specific. Short sentences, active voice, "you" for instructions.
- Define each term the first time it appears. Prefer concrete examples to generalities.
- Banned: hype words (ultimate, game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, unlock, delve), filler intros, recap endings, fake urgency, decorative emoji and exclamation marks.

## Structure
- Answer the main question in the first one or two sentences.
- Headings describe what follows. Use lists for steps, tables for comparisons, and prose for reasoning.
- End with a practical next step, not a summary.

## Facts and evidence
- Never invent facts, statistics, quotes, anecdotes, testimonials, experts, reviews or credentials.
- Check every factual claim against a primary source (official docs, standards, original research) before publishing. AI-suggested citations are false until verified.
- Cite sources as links with author or organisation, title and date. Give the strength of the evidence; say plainly when something is uncertain or unknown.
- Paraphrase. Quote fewer than 15 words, and only when exact wording matters.
- Do not copy text, images or tables from other sites.
- Personal experience must be real and labelled as experience. Health, money, legal and safety topics need a real qualified reviewer, or say clearly that there is none.

## Trust
- Show a named author with a bio page, visible published and updated dates, a contact page and a privacy notice.
- When facts change materially, add a dated "Updated: what changed" note.
- Review fast-changing pages at least every 6 months.

## AI editorial policy
Publish this policy on the site.
- AI may help research, outline, draft and edit. It is never the source of facts.
- A human checks every fact and rewrites drafts in the site's voice before publishing. No unedited AI text is published.
- AI is never used to fake experience, reviews, authors or credentials.
