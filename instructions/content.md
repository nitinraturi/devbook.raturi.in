Load for any public website, docs site or blog. Applies to site copy, docs, blog posts, UI text and release notes.

## First
- CONT-01: Use only real facts, statistics, quotes, anecdotes, testimonials, experts, reviews and credentials. When you lack one, say so or leave it out.
- CONT-02: Check every factual claim against a primary source (official docs, standards, original research) before publishing. AI-suggested citations are false until verified.
- CONT-03: Cut hype words (ultimate, game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, unlock, delve, elevate), filler intros, recap endings, fake urgency, decorative emoji and exclamation marks. Say concretely what something does.
- CONT-04: Answer the main question in the first one or two sentences, then add detail in order of importance. End with a practical next step, not a summary.

## Purpose and voice
- CONT-05: Each page serves a defined reader with one main intent, and leaves them without needing to search again. Add original value: first-hand experience, data, analysis, examples or a clearer explanation than what already exists.
- CONT-06: Write plainly and directly: short sentences, active voice, and "you" for instructions. Aim for about a grade 8 reading level for general audiences.
- CONT-07: UI text uses the user's words, sentence case, and the same term for the same thing everywhere.
- CONT-08: Don't change dates or make cosmetic edits to look fresh. Update a page only when its content changes.

## Evidence
- CONT-09: Cite sources as links with the author or organisation, title and date. Say how strong the evidence is, and say plainly when something is uncertain or unknown.
- CONT-10: Paraphrase. Quote only the minimum, only when exact wording matters, with attribution and a link. Copy text, images or tables from other sites only when their licence allows it.
- CONT-11: Personal experience is real and labelled as experience. Health, money, legal and safety topics (YMYL) need a qualified author or reviewer named on the page, or a clear statement that there is none.

## Trust (E-E-A-T)
- CONT-12: Editorial content (articles, guides, reviews, YMYL pages) has a named author with a bio page showing relevant experience. Every site has an About page, a contact page and a privacy notice.
- CONT-13: Explain how content was made where readers would expect it, such as testing methods, data sources or AI assistance.
- CONT-14: Show visible published and updated dates. When facts change materially, add a dated "Updated: what changed" note.
- CONT-15: Disclose affiliate links, sponsorships and conflicts of interest next to the content they affect.
- CONT-16: Review fast-changing pages at least every 6 months; fix or remove outdated ones.

## AI editorial policy
Publish this policy on the site.
- CONT-17: AI may help research, outline, draft and edit. It is never the source of facts.
- CONT-18: A human checks every fact and rewrites drafts in the site's voice before publishing. Unedited AI text is never published.
- CONT-19: AI is never used to fake experience, reviews, authors or credentials, or to mass-produce pages.
